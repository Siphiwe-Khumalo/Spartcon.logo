01Company background

Engineering services since 2008.

Spartcon has been providing engineering services to clients throughout South Africa since 2008, under the Spartan Group of companies.

Spartcon has the requisite expertise and experience, innovative technologies and depth of resources to maximise operational efficiency and cost effectiveness — while balancing the need for environmental comfort, convenience and safety.

The solutions we deliver are oriented towards maximising asset lifecycle value, ensuring continuous optimum performance of infrastructure, facilities and buildings. This is driven through apredictive, pre-emptive and reactive maintenance approach, so services can be provided with minimum disruption while assuring the comfort of our clients.

We employ engineers, technologists, technicians and skilled trades professionals, with regional offices located strategically throughout the country to provide our complete engineering services.

02Core expertise

Understanding how a facility actually operates.

The heart of our business lies in understanding the operation of facilities, buildings and infrastructure — as well as how these facilities support the people they accommodate.

Spartcon provides outsourced engineering and maintenance solutions nationwide. Our range of expertise, depth of experience and broad geographic coverage enable us to serve either small facilities or large facilities portfolios.

Our staff includes highly skilled professionals with engineering expertise and indispensable technical know-how, who work closely with your personnel to meet key facilities services objectives.

Our engineering managers are registered with ECSA as professional engineers and have served as facilities engineers — bringing the hands-on experience essential to keeping facilities operating at optimum efficiency.

03Maintenance approach

Predictive, pre-emptive, reactive.

Three approaches, applied where each is economic — so minimum disruption is a design decision rather than a hope.

  1. 01

    Predictive Maintenance

    Intervene before failure, based on equipment condition.

    Predictive maintenance identifies deterioration while plant is still running, so work is scheduled against evidence rather than assumption — ensuring services are provided with minimum disruption.

  2. 02

    Pre-emptive Maintenance

    Planned intervals that protect reliability before faults form.

    Pre-emptive maintenance keeps equipment within its intended operating condition to a planned schedule, reducing the number of faults that ever reach the reactive queue.

  3. 03

    Reactive Maintenance

    Structured response when something has already failed.

    Reactive maintenance restores service after a fault or breakdown. It is unavoidable in any estate, so it is organised deliberately — with response pathways and escalation rather than improvisation.
