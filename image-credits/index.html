About the photography on this website

None of the photographs on this website depict Spartcon projects.They are contextual industry and equipment photographs, selected because they genuinely relate to the engineering content beside them. They are not a portfolio of completed work and should not be read as one.

No AI-generated imagery is used anywhere on this site. Every image is a real photograph taken by a named photographer, sourced from Wikimedia Commons under a licence that permits commercial use — CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA. Each image below links to its source page and its licence.

When Spartcon supplies its own project photography, those images will replace this set and will be captioned as genuine Spartcon work.
