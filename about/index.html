01Company background

Two histories, one organisation.

Spartcon (Pty) Ltd is a 100% BEE privately held corporation operating within the Spartan Group of companies.

Our facilities engineering business has provided engineering services to clients throughout South Africa since 2008, under the Spartan Group. Our construction business has been involved in the construction of government, facilities and commercial infrastructuresince 2018.

We are experts in most typical building and civil construction disciplines, having undertaken medium projects spanning from buildings to roads and water infrastructure. Our experience in multidisciplinary projects encompasses industries including mining, residential, factories, corporate offices and warehouse spaces.

We offer professional service from professional engineering and project management throughout the construction value chain. Our diversified construction skills lead us as a noteworthy construction company that is rooted in its reputation of work.

We value sustainable and transparent relationships with our clients over profit — and that is what sets us apart from our competitors.

05Our approach

Partnership from capital planning through to completion.

We endeavour to understand your infrastructure needs to help you mitigate risk and achieve your objectives. We challenge you to give us the opportunity to prove that construction is not a capital-intensive nightmare.

  1. 01

    Partnership

    Our partnership with you starts at your capital planning stage and runs through to project completion. We value sustainable and transparent relationships with our clients over profit.

  2. 02

    Quality Management

    A systematic and consistent Quality Control Programme, tailored to each project or maintenance contract and continually updated. The onsite project manager ensures every facet is followed and maintained.

  3. 03

    Risk Management

    We endeavour to understand your infrastructure needs in order to help you mitigate risk and achieve your objectives — advising on practical techniques and industry best practice to reduce both risk and cost.

  4. 04

    Health & Safety

    Work is planned and executed to OHSA regulations, with minimum engineering standards including SANS and ASME applied across construction and facilities engineering alike.

  5. 05

    Environmental Responsibility

    Our Green Building Programme supports environmental stewardship, assisting facilities towards Green Building Council South Africa certification through engineering design, build, install, operate and maintenance.

  6. 06

    Professional Engineering

    Our construction and engineering managers are registered with ECSA and SACPCMP as professional engineers, bringing the hands-on experience essential to delivering quality projects on time.

  7. 07

    Asset Lifecycle Value

    Solutions are oriented towards maximising asset lifecycle value and ensuring continuous optimum performance of infrastructure, facilities and buildings.

06Standards

The codes we work to.

Our operations platform is built on minimum engineering standards, OEM operational standards and OHSA regulations — applied consistently across construction and facilities engineering.
