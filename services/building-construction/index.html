01Overview

Foundations, structures and steel

Our industry experience ranges from single to multiple storey buildings with complex structural loads. We have in-house construction support solutions — including onsite concrete batch plant, formwork and scaffolding — geared up to equip any concrete project and reduce your cost and risk.

Our diversified construction skills lead us as a noteworthy construction company that is rooted in its reputation of work. We are passionate and committed in building infrastructure that resonates with our company culture and client needs.

Discuss a building construction project

02Capabilities

What this service line covers.

Expand each capability for the specific scope we deliver.

01Engineering Design ServicesEngineering design across architectural, civil, mechanical and electrical disciplines, with quantity surveying support.
  • Design and modelling
  • Project budgeting and estimation
  • Compliance management
  • Architectural and structural design input
02FoundationsFoundation is paramount to any building structure. We specialise in various foundation types, with years of practical experience in concrete structures.
  • Strip, raft and pad foundations
  • Column footing foundations
  • Piling
03Mega StructuresWe have erected structures in industries including mining and commercial buildings, with a team of competent, qualified tradesmen in the building industry.
  • Load-bearing cavity walls
  • Reinforced concrete walls
  • Structural steel support
  • Concrete and masonry structures
04Concrete StructuresWe specialise in constructing vertical, horizontal and transverse columns, and in slab development and erection across multiple structural forms.
  • Design and shuttering of formwork
  • Reinforcing
  • Casting of concrete
  • Testing and signoff
  • Onsite concrete batch plant
05Structural SteelWe operate a full factory facility equipped to design, fabricate and apply corrosion protection to structural steel portals, with onsite fabrication and testing capability.
  • Design
  • Fabrication
  • Erection
  • Sheet cladding
  • Technical compliance and signoff
  • Corrosion protection
06Construction Management & SupportProgramme planning, resource management and contract accounting, with engineering expert trades platform and technical support furnished to the project.
  • Programme planning
  • Trades expertise rationale
  • Resource management
  • Contract accounting
  • Quality Control Programme (QCP)
